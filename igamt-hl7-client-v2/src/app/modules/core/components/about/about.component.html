Tool Information

As of August 2026, the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), is no longer funding the project, and ongoing support for the tools is funded by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), a component of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). The former “NIST Tools” have been rebranded as the Standards & Interoperability Testing Tools (SITT). Prometheus Computing LLC has been contracted by the CDC to maintain, update, and host the HL7 v2 Implementation Guide Authoring and Management Tool (IGAMT).

Funding Sources

IGAMT is supported by the CDC through American Immunization Registry Association (AIRA) as part of a cooperative agreement award.
